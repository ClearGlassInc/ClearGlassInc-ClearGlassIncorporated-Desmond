# Operating Manual

1. Refresh market evidence from lawful public sources.
2. Draft or update a campaign package.
3. Run deterministic validation and tests.
4. Submit claim, compliance, budget, audience, and publication approvals.
5. Export approved assets to the ad platform, CRM, CMS, or outreach tool.
6. Append audit events for each material action.
7. Review weekly performance against qualified leads, booked consultations, proposals, won revenue, recurring revenue, and CAC.
8. Roll back or kill campaigns that exceed risk, budget, complaint, or performance thresholds.
