Under the hood
A pipeline, not a scheduler.

Other tools queue a post. PostLoop produces it — every stage automated, every account on its own cadence.

🗂

Drive-native sourcing

Point it at a Google Drive folder. PostLoop pulls the next clip in the stack, tracks what's been used, and never repeats a reel across an account.

✍️

Auto captions, per language

Hook + body + CTA written for each platform and localized per workspace — Turkish and English branches from the same source clip.

🎵

Music scoring

Trending audio matched and scored against the clip, so every upload rides a sound that's already moving — fitness-music, faceyoga-music, rotated.

⏱

Per-account cadence

Each account runs its own cycle and slot. Countdown to next post, posts-per-cycle, and recovery if a slot is missed — all handled.

📈

Live follower delta

Per-account follower swing, views, likes, saves and engagement update on every cycle so you see what each branch is actually doing.

🧭

Trend wall feedback

The creator wall ranks the best clips in your niche daily and feeds the winning patterns back into caption and music selection.
