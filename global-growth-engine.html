Operating architecture

Not an ad generator. A growth control system.

The engine separates strategy, execution, measurement, and governance. It prioritizes profitable customers over vanity traffic and never assumes that more spend equals better growth.

01 · MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Market scoring

Scores international markets across fit, demand, purchasing power, competition, logistics, regulatory exposure, localization effort, CAC pressure, and repeat-purchase potential.

02 · POSITIONING

Localized persuasion

Builds market-specific value propositions, objections, buying motivations, bundles, trust architecture, language, currency, units, payment expectations, and shipping promises.

03 · ACQUISITION

Multi-channel orchestration

Plans Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Reddit, creator, affiliate, email, SMS, and organic SEO around explicit test hypotheses and unit economics.

04 · CONVERSION

CRO command layer

Diagnoses page friction, trust gaps, offer weakness, product education, checkout loss, shipping anxiety, abandoned carts, pricing psychology, bundles, guarantees, and proof.

05 · RETENTION

Lifecycle revenue

Runs welcome, education, review, cross-sell, reorder, referral, loyalty, and VIP strategies without spam or deceptive urgency.

06 · ANALYTICS

Profit-aware decisions

Tracks CAC, CVR, ROAS, contribution margin, AOV, LTV, repeat purchase rate, refund rate, email revenue, organic revenue, funnel events, and country-level economics.

Decision intelligence

The engine diagnoses the constraint before prescribing the fix.

LOW IMPRESSIONS

Reach problem

Inspect targeting, eligibility, bids, budgets, product-feed approval, keyword volume, and auction access.

LOW CTR

Creative problem

Inspect hook, message-market fit, visual clarity, differentiation, offer strength, and audience relevance.

CLICKS / NO SALES

Conversion problem

Inspect trust, speed, pricing, shipping, localization, product education, mobile UX, payment methods, and checkout friction.

SALES / LOW PROFIT

Economics problem

Inspect acquisition cost, gross margin, discounting, shipping cost, refunds, fulfillment, and contribution margin.

LOW REPEAT

Retention problem

Inspect onboarding, product experience, expectation setting, lifecycle communication, cross-sell logic, replenishment, and loyalty.

GOVERNANCE

Human approval boundary

Agents may analyze, draft, recommend, score, and prepare rollback plans. Budget changes, publishing, messaging, and campaign mutations require explicit approval.
