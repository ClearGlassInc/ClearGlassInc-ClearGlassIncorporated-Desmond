# Final MVP Product Specification - Internal Draft v0.1
## Governed AI Automation Operating Kit

Product Name: Governed AI Automation Operating Kit
Category: Original digital business-automation planning and governance toolkit
Version: v0.1 internal draft

Purpose: Help small businesses, operators, agencies, founders, technical teams identify practical automation opportunities, document workflows, define approval gates, assess operational risk, calculate expected automation value, prepare responsible implementation plan.

Differentiation: Original, structured, security-conscious, governance-oriented planning system. NOT generic bulk prompt bundle.

Components: 12 assets as listed in asset register - all original ClearGlass.

Scope Statement: This product provides general business-process planning, workflow documentation, automation-prioritization, governance, and implementation-readiness materials. It does not provide legal, tax, financial, compliance, security-certification, or custom implementation advice.

Positioning: This toolkit is designed to help teams organize, document, prioritize, and prepare automation initiatives with clearer workflows, defined ownership, approval checkpoints, and practical implementation guidance.

Delivery Format: Digital templates - worksheets, checklists, calculators, SOP templates, roadmap, examples - packaged as versioned ZIP/PDF

Disclaimers: Includes required notice on ROI calculator outputs - Outputs are estimates based on user-entered assumptions. They are planning aids only and do not guarantee savings, financial results, ROI, operational performance, or business outcomes.

Customer Profiles: Approved SMBs, professional-service firms, agencies, founders/operators, technical teams, orgs considering AI-enabled workflows requiring oversight

Not Approved Without Separate Review: Legal/financial/medical/insurance/tax/regulated compliance advice seekers, autonomous high-impact decision systems, cybersecurity certification seekers, prohibited/deceptive/unsafe/illegal/privacy-invasive

Originality: All original ClearGlass-owned, asset register maintained, duplicate-content review PASS, no third-party copied content, no customer data, no secrets

Versioning: v0.1 internal draft, semantic versioning for future
