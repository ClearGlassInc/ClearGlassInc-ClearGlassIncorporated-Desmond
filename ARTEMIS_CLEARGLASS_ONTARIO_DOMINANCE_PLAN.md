## Liquid Glass — the Automated Systems Company

**Liquid Glass** is the automated systems company inside the Artemis
ClearGlass structure. Where ClearGlass sells and operates the Revenue OS for
clients, Liquid Glass **builds, runs and maintains the automation that powers
it** — the proprietary layer competitors cannot copy by hiring a freelancer.

### Mandate

Liquid Glass owns everything that runs without a human touching it:

1. **Agent factory** — proprietary AI agents for lead qualification,
   follow-up sequencing, content drafting and reporting, built on the same
   governed pattern as the commerce OS (read-only analysis → draft → human
   approval → execution).
2. **Automation infrastructure** — CRM pipelines, email/SMS orchestration,
   webhook integrations, scheduled bot loops and monitoring for every
   Revenue OS client install.
3. **Revenue Leakage Audit engine** — the automated scanner and report
   generator behind the flagship audit offer: crawls a prospect's funnel,
   measures response times, and produces the executive report template
   automatically.
4. **Executive analytics** — the weekly revenue reporting and attribution
   dashboards delivered to every client tier.
5. **Cybersecurity and AI governance controls** — hardening, audit logging
   and approval gates applied to every client system, aligned with the
   SENTINEL/PERCIVAL fail-closed agent standards already in this repository.

### Operating rules

Liquid Glass systems inherit the ClearGlass safety model without exception:

* Never fabricate leads, metrics, reviews, urgency or results.
* High-risk actions (pricing, payments, mass outbound) require a recorded
  human approval before execution.
* Every automated action is logged to an append-only audit trail with a
  risk score.
* Client data is segregated per client; no cross-client data reuse.

faster to deploy and better documented. Agencies can copy positioning and
pricing pages in a week; they cannot copy a working, governed, audited
automation platform with case-study data behind it.
