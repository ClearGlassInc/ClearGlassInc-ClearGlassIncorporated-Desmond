# Launch Risk Register - Internal Draft v0.1

Technical Risks: See MVP Design Execution Report Section 6

Commercial Risks: See Section 6

Compliance Risks:
- Risk: Prohibited claims (revenue guarantees, compliance, security certification) appear in listing
  Mitigation: Claims Register with safe vs prohibited wording, QA checklist, owner approval required for all claims, no external representation until internally reviewed and approved

- Risk: Security and Privacy Readiness Checklist misinterpreted as certification
  Mitigation: Label clearly general planning only, disclaimer that it does not provide cybersecurity certification, pen testing, formal assurance, legal advice

- Risk: ROI calculator outputs interpreted as guaranteed savings
  Mitigation: Required notice on all outputs - Outputs are estimates based on user-entered assumptions. They are planning aids only and do not guarantee savings, financial results, ROI, operational performance, or business outcomes.

- Risk: Customer seeks prohibited, deceptive, unsafe, illegal, privacy-invasive automation
  Mitigation: Customer boundaries documented, not approved as primary target, governance checklists include human approval gates, support policy refers to qualified professionals for high-impact decisions

Launch Readiness Risks:
- Risk: Premature publication before QA
  Mitigation: Approval queue - all external actions blocked pending separate approval, completion standard requires verified facts vs assumptions distinguished

- Risk: Storefront not ready
  Mitigation: Technical-readiness report, no modifications without separate approval

Financial Risks:
- Risk: Pricing unsustainable, support burden high
  Mitigation: Pricing options as estimates with delivery cost, support burden, margin assumptions, risks documented, no activation without separate approval

- Risk: Revenue claimed without authenticated records
  Mitigation: BLOCKED - no revenue reporting without authenticated financial records per authorization
