# Daily Organic Execution Playbook

## Pre-publish (5 min)
1. Pull today’s pack from `marketing/output/organic/`
2. Tighten hook if needed
3. Confirm CTA points to owned asset or conversation

## Publish sequence
1. X / Threads first (short, high-velocity)
2. LinkedIn version (longer or carousel)
3. Strategic comments on 3–5 relevant accounts in the niche

## Post-publish (10 min)
- Reply to every meaningful comment within 2 hours
- Save high-performing replies for future content
- Log any inbound DMs or clicks

## Repurposing rule
One idea → minimum 3 formats before the week ends.
