WHY THIS REQUIRES A HUMAN

Supplier registration is a sworn corporate act with identity verification.

PSPC's supplier framework requires the founder to authenticate, attest to the Integrity Declaration, supply the CRA Business Number, and certify financial and ownership information. SAP Ariba and Buyandsell account creation require email verification on a corporate domain.

HANDOFF SEQUENCE

Six steps to listed federal supplier status.

Confirm prerequisites are complete

Federal registration depends on prior handoffs:

  • Ontario Certificate of Incorporation issued
  • CRA Business Number and GST/HST registration active
  • Banking established and direct-deposit form ready
  • Public site live at www.clearglassinc.com
OWNER · FOUNDER · GATING CHECK

Obtain a Procurement Business Number (PBN)

Apply through the Supplier Registration Information (SRI) service to obtain the 15-character PBN that identifies ClearGlass Inc. across federal procurement systems.

OWNER · FOUNDER

Create SAP Ariba supplier account

Register on the federal SAP Ariba supplier portal. Map the GSIN / NAICS codes that fit the ClearGlass capability set — cybersecurity services, AI consulting, information technology professional services, and management consulting.

OWNER · FOUNDER

Sign the Integrity Declaration

Read and sign the PSPC Integrity Declaration, confirming that no officer, director, or affiliate has been convicted of a listed offence under the Integrity Regime. File the signed declaration with corporate records.

OWNER · FOUNDER

Register for security clearance pre-screening

Initiate the Contract Security Program registration through the Canadian Industrial Security Directorate (CISD) so that future bids requiring designated organization screening can move quickly. Identify the Company Security Officer (CSO).

OWNER · FOUNDER · CSO

Mirror onto Ontario and municipal rosters

Use the same supplier identity to register on MERX, Bonfire, and major Ontario municipal vendor portals (City of Toronto, Region of Halton, City of Burlington, City of Hamilton). Mark this handoff complete on the procurement readiness hub.

CONTROL RULE

No bid without a verified profile.

ClearGlass Inc. does not respond to a federal, provincial, or municipal solicitation until the supplier profile on the relevant portal is verified, accurate, and aligned with the public corporate footprint. Every bid is traceable to a single, current supplier record of truth.
