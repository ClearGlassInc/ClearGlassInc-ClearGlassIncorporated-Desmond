The procurement landscape as it stands

Two parallel procurement channels remain open and compliant for ClearGlass Inc. as an Ontario-incorporated cybersecurity and AI governance corporation operating from Burlington.

FEDERAL · GOVERNMENT OF CANADA

Federal procurement remains accessible

Federal procurement systems and supplier registration remain accessible through compliant procurement channels — including SAP Ariba, GETS, and the Supplier Registration Information (SRI) framework administered by Public Services and Procurement Canada.

PROVINCIAL · ONTARIO

Ontario prioritizes compliant domestic suppliers

Ontario procurement continues prioritizing compliant domestic suppliers and structured procurement readiness — through the Ontario Vendor of Record program, MERX, Bonfire, and direct municipal vendor rosters across the Greater Golden Horseshoe.
