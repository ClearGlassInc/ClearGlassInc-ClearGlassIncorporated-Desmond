# ClearGlass Weekly Execution Pack
## Governed AI, Operational Dependency, and Cryptographic Readiness

**Purpose:** Client-ready operating assets for Ontario organizations adopting AI, depending on third-party technology, and needing defensible evidence of control.

**Positioning:** ClearGlass makes hidden operational dependency visible, separates evidence from assumption, and gives leadership a tested plan to keep critical services running when a provider, platform, model, identity service, or jurisdiction changes.

## Asset 1 — AI Agent Access Control Standard

Use before approving an AI agent with access to internal systems, data, APIs, SaaS, browsers, command lines, or workflows.

**Primary controls:** ownership, approved purpose, unique identity, least privilege, managed secrets, data classification, human authorization, tool/domain allow-lists, immutable activity logging, monitoring, adversarial testing, vendor assurance, change management, and tested termination/manual fallback.

**Production gate:** no deployment until ownership, purpose, permissions, data flows, human approval gates, logging/monitoring, security testing, kill-switch/manual fallback, and residual-risk acceptance are evidenced.

[Detailed standard](AI_AGENT_ACCESS_CONTROL_STANDARD.md)

## Asset 2 — AI Dependency & Recovery Review

A leadership-level diagnostic that maps critical AI/technology dependencies, verifies evidence, stress-tests disruption scenarios, and produces a 30/60/90-day recovery roadmap.

**Packages:**

| Package | Best fit | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Rapid Review | Single critical workflow | Dependency map, priority risks, 30-day actions |
| Operational Review | Multiple AI workflows/business unit | Evidence register, stress tests, 90-day roadmap |
| Assurance Retainer | Ongoing AI expansion/regulated environment | Monthly change review, risk reporting, control validation |

[Detailed service scope](AI_DEPENDENCY_RECOVERY_REVIEW.md)

## Asset 3 — Post-Quantum Cryptographic Inventory

Use as an adjacent resilience service where clients have long-lived data, regulated obligations, industrial technology, or complex supplier dependencies.

**Immediate objective:** inventory cryptographic assets and dependencies, identify long-lived exposure, assess crypto-agility, collect vendor commitments, and establish a governed migration sequence.

[Detailed readiness scope](POST_QUANTUM_CRYPTOGRAPHIC_INVENTORY.md)

## Operating sequence

1. Apply the AI Agent Access Control Standard before approving agent access.
2. Position the AI Dependency & Recovery Review as the leadership diagnostic.
3. Introduce the Post-Quantum Cryptographic Inventory for clients with long-lived confidentiality, regulated obligations, industrial systems, or complex vendor dependencies.
4. Collect only verifiable evidence. Mark unverified statements as assumptions and assign an owner and due date.
5. Report progress through measurable control coverage, not generic maturity claims.

## Evidence discipline

Every material claim should be classified as one of:

- **Verified** — supported by an identified evidence source.
- **Derived** — calculated or correlated from verified evidence.
- **Assumption** — not yet verified; must have an owner and due date.
- **Unknown** — evidence is unavailable or the dependency has not yet been identified.

Do not convert assumptions or unknowns into compliance, certification, security, or resilience claims.

## Client-facing outcome

The engagement is successful when leadership can show, with evidence:

- which AI-enabled workflows are business-critical;
- which vendors, data flows, identities, and tools those workflows depend on;
- what authority agents and integrations possess;
- what fails when a key dependency is unavailable or changed;
- how access is revoked and operations are recovered; and
- which named leaders own remaining risks and corrective actions.

**ClearGlass doctrine:** Evidence before assertion. Provenance before convenience. Human accountability by design.
