# Privacy Notice Template — Customer Profile Intake

Use this notice before collecting any customer personal information.

---

## ClearGlass Inc. Customer Profile Intake Notice

Before we collect any personal information, here is exactly what we are requesting and why.

### 1. What information we are requesting

We are requesting only the information needed to respond to your service request:

- Your name or business name
- Your preferred contact method
- Your contact detail for that method
- The service you are requesting
- Any project details you choose to provide
- Your explicit consent to continue

Optional fields will be clearly marked optional.

We will not ask for passwords, unnecessary identity documents, banking credentials, medical information, or sensitive personal attributes.

### 2. Why it is needed

This information is needed to understand your request, contact you, provide the requested service, and maintain necessary service records.

### 3. How it will be used

Your information will be used only for:

- Responding to your inquiry
- Delivering or preparing the requested service
- Scheduling communication
- Managing consent, correction, withdrawal, or deletion requests
- Maintaining limited compliance records

It will not be used for unrelated marketing, resale, profiling, or hidden data collection.

### 4. How long it will be retained

Service-intake information will be retained only while needed to provide the requested service, then deleted or anonymized within 12 months after the last interaction unless a shorter period is requested or a legal/business retention obligation applies.

Consent and deletion-request logs may be retained for up to 24 months as compliance evidence unless legal obligations require otherwise.

### 5. Whether it will be shared with third parties

We will not sell your information.

We may share limited information only with service providers required to deliver the requested service, or where required by law. Any required service-provider use should be limited to the purpose described here.

### 6. How you can decline, withdraw consent, or request deletion

You may decline optional fields, stop the intake process, withdraw consent, request correction, or request deletion by contacting:

`privacy@clearglassinc.com`

Privacy policy:

`https://www.clearglassinc.com/privacy.html`

Deletion may be subject to legal retention requirements.

### Explicit consent

Please confirm before continuing:

> I understand what information ClearGlass Inc. is requesting, why it is needed, how it will be used, how long it will be retained, whether it may be shared, and how I can decline, withdraw consent, or request deletion. I consent to proceed with customer-profile intake.

Consent must be recorded as `yes` before collection continues.
