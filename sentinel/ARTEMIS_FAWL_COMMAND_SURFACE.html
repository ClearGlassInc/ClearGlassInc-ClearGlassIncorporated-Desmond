Enforced invariants (code + CI)

  • Illegal transitions rejected — the lifecycle only advances along an explicit allowed-transition table.
  • Level-4 never executes; AI-originated level ≥ 2 can never self-authorize.
  • Kill switch freezes mutating automation while observation continues.
  • Single-use capabilities — one action, one target, short TTL, redeemable once.
  • Failed verification ⇒ rollback ⇒ escalate; malicious telemetry ⇒ quarantine.
  • Tamper-evident — every transition and decision hash-chained in the audit log.

Where this sits

  • Governance spine over the PHOENIX recovery engines (phoenix.py / selfheal.py): they decide what to fix, ARTEMIS // FAWL decides whether it may run.
  • Pure stdlib; all side effects flow through injected executor / verifier adapters — safe to test and embed.
  • Ships as sentinel/sentinel/artemis_fawl.py with tests/test_artemis_fawl.py and the Phoenix Self-Heal CI gate.
