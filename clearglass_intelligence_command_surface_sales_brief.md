# ClearGlass Intelligence Command Surface

## Positioning Line

> **ClearGlass turns public-source noise into decision-grade intelligence for organizations operating at the edge of acceptable risk.**

## Sales Positioning

ClearGlass Intelligence Command Surface is a premium enterprise security intelligence platform for government, financial institutions, and large enterprise buyers.

The platform communicates trust, urgency, and operational clarity for sophisticated decision-makers who have already seen every security vendor pitch. It is designed to help CISOs, security teams, and leadership quickly understand exposure, prioritize threats, and plan action before risk becomes incident.

ClearGlass presents lawful open-source intelligence, risk signals, and defensive context in a clean, executive-ready format. Every module emphasizes provenance, confidence, freshness, and impact so buyers can trust the system as transparent, auditable, and built for real operational use.
