# ClearGlass Website Homepage Unification Blueprint

This blueprint defines how every ClearGlass page should match the homepage as one premium brand system while preserving existing content.

## Pages that need to be matched to the homepage

Apply the homepage system to every indexable marketing, product, offer, legal, blog, investor, and operations page. Priority pages are:

- Core brand pages: `index.html`, `intelligence-platform.html`, `intelligence-command-surface.html`, `platform-command-center.html`, `authority-network.html`.
- Product pages: every page listed in the final catalog below.
- Service and offer pages: `offers/index.html`, `offers/autonomous-threat-modeling.html`, `offers/phipa-readiness.html`, `offers/security-quick-audit.html`, `offers/hardening-sprint.html`, `pricing.html`, `web-design.html`.
- Trust and operations pages: `legal/*.html`, `operations/*.html`, `investors/index.html`, `blog/*.html`.

## Homepage elements to reuse across the site

- Fixed glass navigation with ClearGlassInc. 2040 identity, compact product dropdown, premium contact CTA, and mobile drawer.
- Prism-light brand language: white/off-white surfaces, dark text, cyan/violet/mint accents, soft aurora glow, restrained lift on hover, and glass cards.
- Homepage section rhythm: hero, mission strip, principle/manifesto, values or proof cards, dark capability section, product/command surface, timeline or process, founder/trust block, final CTA.
- Homepage voice: concise, executive, governed, precise, security-aware, and premium without hype.
- Homepage CTAs: primary dark pill for briefing/contact and secondary glass pills for product exploration.

## Navigation standard

- Use one primary nav pattern everywhere: Vision, Services, Products, Founder, Contact.
- Products dropdown and mobile menu must mirror the complete product catalog.
- Product labels should be short and scan-first; sublabels should explain category or job-to-be-done.
- Blog/Insights remains a content destination, separated from products.
- Existing local page controls may remain, but the global bar is the unifying navigation layer.

## Headings, spacing, and flow standard

- Eyebrows use uppercase mono labels with wide tracking.
- Primary page headings use the homepage serif scale and emphasize one phrase with strong weight.
- Body copy stays short, grounded, and operational; no fabricated status, urgency, inventory, or proof.
- Sections use the homepage `.sect` vertical rhythm with centered containers and 20px card grids.
- Product cards use consistent era/kicker, heading, description, feature bullets, and CTA order.
- Dark sections should be reserved for capability, command, security, or operational depth.

## Rewritten global page structure

1. **Hero** — page-specific product/service promise, one primary CTA, one secondary CTA, and a concise proof/operating-state rail.
2. **Mission strip** — one sentence connecting the page to ClearGlass strategy, security, automation, and operations.
3. **Principle** — a short manifesto statement or operating doctrine.
4. **Capabilities** — three to six cards using homepage card language.
5. **System / workflow** — how the product or service works, preferably as a process/timeline or command panel.
6. **Governance / trust** — permissions, human approval, auditability, security boundaries, or evidence handling when relevant.
7. **Related products** — connected catalog links that keep users inside the ClearGlass ecosystem.
8. **Founder / credibility** — founder, operating model, or organizational proof appropriate to the page.
9. **Final CTA** — “Request a Strategic Brief”, “Open [Product]”, or “Review Capabilities”.
