Reality check

These are cues—not secret commands

ChatGPT does not have a universal hidden command language for these phrases. They work because concise labels communicate intent. Results still depend on your context, constraints, source material, and requested output. A label such as FACT-CHECK asks for verification; it does not guarantee live browsing or make an unsupported claim true.

Likewise, asking for “chain of thought,” “force trace,” or “metacognitive” output cannot guarantee access to private internal reasoning. Ask instead for a concise rationale, assumptions, calculations, evidence, or a verification checklist you can inspect.

🧠 Foundational shortcuts

Compress, clarify, organize

Use these when the problem is information shape—not subject-matter depth.

ELI5 / ELI12 / ELI15Explain for a chosen level in plain language.ELI12: Explain blockchain technology.
TL;DRCondense long content into key points.TL;DR: [paste content]
STEP-BY-STEPTurn a task into an ordered procedure.Build a portfolio site, step-by-step.
CHECKLISTCreate a checkable action list.CHECKLIST: Launch a YouTube channel.
EXEC SUMMARYSurface decisions, risks, and implications.EXEC SUMMARY: [paste report]
OUTLINEBuild a structured content skeleton.OUTLINE: Content marketing strategy.
FRAMEWORKOrganize a reusable approach.FRAMEWORK: Weekly time management.

✍️ Tone & style modifiers

Control the voice

Name the audience, tone, and constraints rather than hoping the model infers them.

JARGON / JARGONIZEUse domain terminology where it improves precision.JARGON: Cloud migration benefits.
HUMANIZEUse natural, conversational phrasing.HUMANIZE: This thank-you email.
AUDIENCE: [TYPE]Adapt knowledge and examples to the reader.AUDIENCE: Teenagers — healthy eating.
TONE: [STYLE]Set a friendly, formal, direct, or humorous voice.TONE: Friendly — welcome message.
SIMPLIFYReduce complexity without removing the core meaning.SIMPLIFY: Machine-learning concepts.
AMPLIFYAdd energy, specificity, and momentum.AMPLIFY: Product launch announcement.

👤 Roles & reasoning

Choose a lens, then demand evidence

ACT AS: [ROLE]Apply a professional lens; it does not confer credentials.ACT AS: Career coach — resume tips.
ROLE · TASK · FORMATDefine the job as a compact contract.ROLE: Editor | TASK: Review | FORMAT: Table
MULTI-PERSPECTIVEPresent credible competing viewpoints.MULTI-PERSPECTIVE: Remote work.
EXPERT MODE / CONSULTANTRequest deeper terminology and strategic advice.CONSULTANT: Improve retention.
FEYNMAN TECHNIQUEExplain simply, expose gaps, then rebuild.FEYNMAN: AI language models.
FIRST PRINCIPLESSeparate assumptions from basic constraints.FIRST PRINCIPLES: Reduce expenses.
DELIBERATE THINKINGAsk for considered analysis before a concise result.Analyze options; state assumptions and checks.
BIAS CHECKInspect framing, missing groups, and weak assumptions.SYSTEMATIC BIAS CHECK: [statement]
DIALECTICStage claim, counterclaim, and synthesis.DIALECTIC: AI and human jobs.
DEVIL'S ADVOCATEStress the strongest counterarguments.DEVIL'S ADVOCATE: This proposal.
METACOGNITIVERequest an inspectable plan and self-check—not private reasoning.Show assumptions, method, and validation.
CHAIN OF THOUGHTPrefer a concise rationale or verifiable calculation.Give the answer, key steps, and checks.

📊 Analytical shortcuts

Interrogate the decision

SWOTStrengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.SWOT: Launch an online course.
COMPARECompare against explicit criteria.COMPARE: A vs B | Criteria: cost, risk.
CONTEXT STACKLayer goal, constraints, evidence, and history.CONTEXT STACK: AI in education.
3-PASS ANALYSISReview structure, evidence, then polish.3-PASS ANALYSIS: Business pitch.
PRE-MORTEMAssume failure and identify likely causes.PRE-MORTEM: Product launch.
ROOT CAUSETrace symptoms to testable causes.ROOT CAUSE: Traffic decline.
IMPACT ANALYSISMap first- and second-order consequences.IMPACT: Remote-work policy.
RISK MATRIXRank likelihood, impact, controls, and owners.RISK MATRIX: New market entry.

📋 Output-formatting tokens

Specify the deliverable

FORMAT AS: [TYPE]Request a table, list, JSON, memo, or slides.FORMAT AS: Table — compare EVs.
BEGIN WITH / END WITHControl the opening and close.BEGIN WITH: Decision | END WITH: Risks
REWRITE AS: [STYLE]Transform existing text without changing facts.REWRITE AS: Casual blog post.
TEMPLATECreate a reusable fill-in structure.TEMPLATE: Email newsletter.
HIERARCHYOrder information by importance or dependency.HIERARCHY: Project priorities.

🛡️ Simulation & quality control

Review the answer—not the model's hidden process

REFLECTIVE MODE / EVAL-SELFScore the draft against explicit criteria and revise.Score accuracy, clarity, utility; then revise.
NO AUTOPILOTReject clichés and demand distinct options.Give 10 non-overlapping ad concepts.
MULTI-AGENT SIMULATIONSimulate named roles; it may still be one model.Customer | Support | Compliance review.
FRICTION / STRESS TESTAdd adverse constraints to test resilience.STRESS TEST: Strategy at half the budget.
SCENARIO PLANNINGExplore plausible futures and indicators.Optimistic, base, adverse scenarios.
GUARDRAILState hard boundaries and exclusions.GUARDRAIL: Cite sources; flag uncertainty.
FACT-CHECK / TRUTH GATERequest citations and distinguish verified from unknown.Verify each claim; link primary sources.
PEER REVIEW / CRITIC LOOPApply a rubric, list defects, and produce a revision.PEER REVIEW: Research method.
FORCE TRACEUse a decision log: sources, assumptions, result, confidence.Return evidence and an audit table.
FAILSAFEAsk for an instruction-compliance check; enforce critical rules outside AI.List unmet requirements before finalizing.

🧪 Experimental labels

Use creatively, not literally. The following names are memorable prompt labels, but they are not documented universal ChatGPT controls. Define the behavior you want after the label; otherwise the model may interpret it inconsistently.

THOUGHT_WIPE — restart from stated facts

TOKEN_MASKING — ignore specified fields

ECHO-FREEZE — preserve named assumptions

TEMPERATURE_SIM — request safer or bolder variants

TRIGGER_CHAIN — execute named phases

FORK_CONTEXT — explore independent branches

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE — assume no reader context

SHADOW_PRO — decompose the problem

SELF_PATCH — find and revise defects

AUTO_MODULATE — adapt depth by section

SAFE_LATCH — repeat and preserve boundaries

ZERO_IMPRINT — inspect bias; it cannot erase training

QUANT_CHAIN — show formulas and verify arithmetic

Turn one response into a process

Stack shortcuts for stronger prompts

The most reliable order is context → role/task → constraints → quality checks → format. Pipe symbols are just readable separators.

CONTEXT: B2B SaaS startup raising a seed round
AUDIENCE: Investors
TASK: Create an executive summary
GUARDRAIL: Use only the supplied metrics; flag missing evidence
FORMAT AS: 6 presentation slides with speaker notes
ACT AS: Project manager | PRE-MORTEM | RISK MATRIX
GOAL: Launch the customer portal in 30 days
CONSTRAINTS: 4 engineers, fixed budget, no weekend work
FORMAT AS: Table with risk, signal, likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner
SIMPLIFY | HUMANIZE | FORMAT AS: Bullet points
AUDIENCE: New customer
TONE: Friendly and direct
GUARDRAIL: Preserve every date, price, and commitment in the source
The one formula to bookmark

Good prompts beat magic words

Goal + context + evidence + constraints + audience + output format + quality bar. Shortcuts make that formula faster to write. They do not replace it.
