The story

When power erases the evidence, one ledger can expose it.

After Eleanor Vance's murder, an analyst tracing suspicious financial activity uncovers a hidden network of influence. The Harrow Group has a plan, and Alexander Kane's handwritten ledger may be the only evidence capable of unraveling it. But the people seeking the truth are running out of time.

This is a fictional narrative. Names, organizations, and events in the story are presented as fiction, not factual allegations.
