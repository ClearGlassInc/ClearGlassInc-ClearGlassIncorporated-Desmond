# ClearGlass Website Unification Blueprint

## Pages that need to be matched to the homepage

All indexable marketing, product, offer, legal, operations handoff, and blog pages should inherit the homepage system. Priority pages are the top-level product and conversion pages: Guardian, BLUEDESK, ClearGlass NEXUS v12, ClearPulse, Flowsint, Command Center, Conduit, Artemis IV Core, Artemis OS, Artemis Self-Evolving Platform, Artemis AI Cyber Intelligence Platform, Air Systems Control, SATS Digital Twin, Counter-UAS Commercialization OS, Procurement Legal Tech, Traffic Enforcement, SMB Suite, SMB Cyber Trust Kit, Web Design & Development, Side Store, Revenue Engine, StegoForge, PERCIVAL OS, and SENTINEL.

## Homepage elements to reuse across the site

- Fixed premium glass navigation with ClearGlassInc. 2040 identity, concise anchors, complete Products dropdown, and a dark Contact CTA.
- Light prism background language: aurora glow, cursor glow, subtle scan lines, glass cards, crystal accent text, and mono overlines.
- Section sequence: hero promise, mission/context strip, proof cards, product/system grid, governance/trust layer, founder or authority layer, and final contact CTA.
- Button system: dark primary CTA for committed actions, glass secondary CTA for exploration, crystal accent only for high-emphasis moments.
- Card system: rounded 18–24px cards, thin glass borders, soft shadow lift, concise headline, explanatory body, and direct action link.

## Navigation standards

- Keep the primary nav simple: Vision, Technology, Products, Services, Founder, Contact.
- Put the full product catalog inside Products on desktop and mobile.
- Use exact product names and short descriptors; avoid clever labels that hide meaning.
- Keep product labels scan-friendly: product name first, category/subtitle second.
- Preserve existing page URLs and do not remove legacy pages; route users through the unified global topbar.

## Heading, spacing, and flow standards

- Use Cormorant Garamond for hero and section headings, Urbanist for body/UI, and IBM Plex Mono for overlines, status text, and metadata.
- Start sections with a mono overline, a serif heading, and a restrained explanatory paragraph.
- Apply homepage spacing rhythm: 80–140px vertical section padding and 16–24px grid gaps.
- Use one primary CTA per section, supported by one secondary CTA only when needed.
- Keep tone premium, specific, governed, and operational: clear claims, no fabricated status, no false urgency.

## Rewritten global page structure

1. **Global navigation** — ClearGlassInc. 2040 brand, Products dropdown, Services, Founder, Contact.
2. **Hero** — one sharp promise, one credibility paragraph, primary CTA, secondary context link.
3. **Mission strip** — why this page exists and what operating problem it solves.
4. **Capability cards** — 3–6 cards using the homepage card system.
5. **Workflow / architecture** — how the product or service works from intake to outcome.
6. **Governance and trust** — approvals, auditability, privacy/security boundaries, limitations.
7. **Related products** — direct paths into adjacent ClearGlass systems.
8. **Final CTA** — Contact or launch the relevant product page.
9. **Generated internal links** — preserve and regenerate with `tools/internal_links.py` when page graph changes.
