# LinkedIn — connect + soft follow-up

Warmer and lower-risk than cold email: no CASL message needed for a plain
connection request, and you build a relationship before pitching. Keep it human.

---

## Connection request (≤300 chars, no pitch)

Hi {{first_name}} — I work with Ontario {{industry}} teams on practical security
& PHIPA readiness (Burlington-based). Came across {{company}} and thought I'd
connect. No pitch — happy to share useful resources if ever helpful.

---

## Follow-up message (only after they accept, 1–2 days later)

Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}. Genuinely no sales pitch — I just publish
free, practical stuff for teams handling sensitive data.

If it's useful, here's our free {{PHIPA / M365 hardening}} checklist:
{{https://www.clearglassinc.com/offers/}}

If you ever want a second set of eyes on your security posture, we do a fixed-fee
$249 read-only Quick-Audit — but no rush, and no obligation.

---

## Notes
- One follow-up, then let it breathe. Relationship > conversion on LinkedIn.
- Post useful content consistently; inbound beats outbound here.
- Track which posts/links drive `store.html` visits in your analytics.
