## Is your organization PHIPA-ready?

Ontario's *Personal Health Information Protection Act* holds you accountable for
safeguarding personal health information (PHI). A single avoidable breach means
IPC reporting, patient notification, reputational damage — and questions you
can't answer after the fact.

**ClearGlass Inc.** helps Ontario health-sector teams find and close the gaps

### The 5 questions most teams can't confidently answer
1. Where exactly does PHI live, and who can reach it today?
2. Is access logged and reviewed — would you detect misuse?
3. Is MFA enforced on every account that touches PHI?
4. Do you have a tested breach-response plan (not just a document)?
5. Are your vendors and integrations contractually and technically safe?
