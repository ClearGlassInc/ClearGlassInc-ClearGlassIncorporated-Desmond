# Outreach drafts

Human-reviewed, human-sent outreach assets for ClearGlass Inc.'s security &
compliance offers. These are **drafts to adapt and send yourself** — not an
automated campaign. Auto-sending cold email at volume is both ineffective and,
in Canada, illegal.

## Read this first — CASL (Canada's Anti-Spam Law)

Before sending any commercial electronic message, every draft here must:

1. **Have a lawful basis to contact the recipient.** Prefer **express consent**
   or an **existing business relationship**. CASL's implied-consent path for a
   business email "conspicuously published" without a no-solicitation notice is
   narrow — keep volumes low, lists targeted, and never scrape.
2. **Identify the sender** — your name, *ClearGlass Inc.*, Burlington, Ontario,
   and a working reply address.
3. **Offer a clear opt-out** that you honour within 10 business days.
4. **Use a truthful subject line** — no bait.

Penalties for getting this wrong are real (up to $1M/individual). When unsure,
send fewer, warmer, more relevant messages.

## How to use

1. Replace every `{{placeholder}}` (recipient, your reply email, your booking
   link/`store.html`).
2. Make it specific — reference something real about the recipient. Generic = ignored.
3. Send 5–10 highly-targeted messages, measure replies in your analytics +
   inbox, iterate. Volume is not the goal; relevance is.
