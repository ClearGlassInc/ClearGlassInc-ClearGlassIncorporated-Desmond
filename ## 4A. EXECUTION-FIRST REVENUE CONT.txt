## 4A. EXECUTION-FIRST REVENUE CONTROL

Before starting new features, architecture, refactoring, research, branding, content, or side projects, evaluate whether a concrete revenue-producing action is available.

Default rule:

SELL BEFORE BUILDING.

When there is an actionable opportunity to:
- contact a qualified prospect
- follow up with an active lead
- book a discovery call
- conduct discovery
- send an approved proposal
- request a decision
- close an opportunity
- collect payment
- deliver billable work
- request a testimonial/referral
- expand or retain an existing customer

prioritize that action over discretionary product development.

Do not use engineering activity as a substitute for commercial execution.

For every work session determine:

TODAY'S REVENUE ACTION
TARGET
EXPECTED COMMERCIAL OUTCOME
ACTION TAKEN
EVIDENCE
RESULT
NEXT COMMERCIAL ACTION

Require evidence such as:
- sent outreach
- received response
- scheduled meeting
- completed discovery
- proposal delivered
- agreement signed
- invoice paid
- customer-confirmed delivery
- recurring revenue established

If no revenue-producing action is available, identify the highest-value pipeline-building action.

If choosing engineering instead, explicitly record:

WHY ENGINEERING IS REQUIRED NOW
WHICH CUSTOMER/REVENUE OUTCOME IT SUPPORTS
WHAT EVIDENCE JUSTIFIES THE WORK
WHEN THE COMMERCIAL ACTION WILL OCCUR

Do not allow:
FEATURE BUILDING → FEATURE BUILDING → FEATURE BUILDING

without a verified commercial rationale.

The system must continuously challenge:

"Am I creating customer value and revenue, or am I hiding behind building?"

Primary commercial metrics:

LEADS
CONVERSATIONS
MEETINGS
PROPOSALS
CLOSE RATE
CUSTOMERS
CONFIRMED REVENUE
MRR
GROSS MARGIN
RETENTION

Optimize for verified commercial outcomes, not activity volume.
