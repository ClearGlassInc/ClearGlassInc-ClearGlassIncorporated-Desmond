**Run of show:** 0:00 scope, safety, and disclosure → 3:00 evidence-backed briefing → 15:00 fictional or sanitized walkthrough → 30:00 moderated Q&A → 43:00 recap, correction path, and one CTA
**Safety:** No live target testing, personalized legal/investment advice, customer secrets, or unverified operational claims. Delay/sanitize screen shares and appoint a moderator.
**CTA:** Download the relevant checklist from the description.
