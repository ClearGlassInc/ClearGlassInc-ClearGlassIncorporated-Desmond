# ClearGlassInc — 90-Day YouTube Growth Strategy

## 1. Defensible niche and promise

**Niche:** evidence-led operational intelligence for small and mid-market leaders who must make AI, cyber-risk, crypto-infrastructure, and automation decisions without hype. The channel does not compete as a general news feed or speculative trading channel. It turns complex systems into auditable decisions, checklists, and governed implementation patterns.

**Audience promise:** “In every episode, ClearGlassInc will expose one hidden system, show the evidence, and give you a safe action you can execute this week.”

**Competitive advantage**

1. A Canadian business and privacy lens with globally useful implementation guidance.
2. A repeatable **Claim → Evidence → Decision → Control** format rather than opinion-only commentary.
3. Technical depth translated into executive decisions, with downloadable controls and templates.
4. Clear separation between deployed facts, demonstrations, simulations, and target-state designs.
5. Governed automation: models may propose; deterministic policy and accountable humans approve consequential action.

**Editorial boundaries:** no price predictions, exploit walkthroughs against live targets, malware, credential theft, fabricated case studies, undisclosed sponsorships, or claims that ClearGlassInc Artemis/Palantir capabilities are deployed without evidence. Educational security demonstrations use isolated, owned labs.

## 6. Conversion funnel

**Video → relevant lead magnet → permission-based email sequence → fit assessment → consultation → proposal/product.** Each description uses one primary CTA and one secondary “watch next” link.

Canonical link form:

```text
https://www.clearglassinc.com/resources/<asset>?
utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic_video&utm_campaign=yt_launch_90d&
utm_content=<video_id>_<placement>&utm_term=<pillar>
```

Allowed placements: `description_top`, `pinned_comment`, `end_screen`, `community`, `live`. Preserve no sensitive data in UTMs. Store source/medium/campaign/content with consent status in the CRM. Report aggregate conversion; restrict raw contact data.

**Five-email sequence:** (1) deliver asset immediately; (2) next-day implementation example; (3) day 3 common failure; (4) day 6 related case pattern, clearly identified as real or illustrative; (5) day 9 optional consultation. Every message includes identity, reason received, privacy link, and unsubscribe.

Some links may be affiliate links; if you use one, ClearGlassInc may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Recommendations are selected independently. Paid promotion is labeled in the video and with YouTube’s paid-promotion setting.”

## 7. Weekly scorecard and decision rules

| Metric | Definition | First 90-day diagnostic band (not a promise) | Action |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Impressions | YouTube-reported eligible thumbnail impressions | Trend by traffic source | Low impressions + good CTR/retention: improve topic/packaging distribution, not content quality claim |
| Impressions CTR | Views from counted impressions ÷ impressions | 3–7% working range | Below 3% after ≥1,000 impressions: test one materially different thumbnail/title |
| 30-second retention | Viewers remaining at 0:30 ÷ starts | ≥65% target | Below 55% after ≥200 views: rewrite cold open; remove preamble |
| Average view duration | Watch time ÷ views | ≥35% of long-form runtime | Diagnose exact dips; change one structural variable |
| Returning viewers | YouTube Analytics returning-viewer estimate | Four-week upward trend | Strengthen named series and next-episode promise |
| Subscribers/1,000 views | Net subscribers ÷ views × 1,000 | 5–15 target | Low: align CTA with fulfilled value; never gate core answer |
| Leads | Valid consented inquiries attributed to content | 1–3 per 1,000 relevant views hypothesis | Audit CTA/landing relevance and form friction |
| Sales | Closed-won orders attributed under documented model | Report count and CAD net | Do not optimize on tiny samples or claim causality from last click alone |
| Revenue/video | Attributed net revenue ÷ published videos | Cohort by 30/60/90 days | Include refunds, sponsor/affiliate disclosures, and production cost |

**Review every Monday:** compare each release at 24h, 7d, and 28d against its traffic-source and format cohort—not a universal benchmark.

**Rules**

- Change a weak thumbnail/title once after ≥1,000 impressions or 7 days, whichever comes first; record original, variant, timestamp, and outcome. Do not repeatedly churn.
- If 30-second retention is <55% after ≥200 starts, rebuild the first 30 seconds for the next two episodes: result first, stakes second, identity last.
- Expand a topic when two videos exceed the channel’s 28-day median for both qualified watch time and lead conversion; produce a three-part series.
- Pause a format only after four comparable attempts. Discontinue after six if it remains below 70% of channel median watch time and produces no compensating lead/authority result.
- A breakout is not a strategy change until a related follow-up also outperforms.
- Never buy, barter, automate, or incentivize views, subscribers, comments, likes, or watch time. Never use misleading metadata or engagement bait.

## 8. Current YouTube policy and monetization verification

**Research checkpoint: 2026-08-01.** The following official help pages are the publication-time verification set. Platform rules can change; the producer must reopen them during final QA and record reviewer/date in the episode evidence register. Network access was unavailable in the build environment, so no claim below should be treated as a substitute for a fresh pre-publication review.

- **Expanded YPP entry tier (eligible countries/regions):** 500 subscribers, three valid public uploads in 90 days, plus either 3,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 3 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days. This unlocks eligible fan-funding/shopping features, not full ad-revenue sharing. **Full ad revenue tier:** 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days. Channels must also satisfy policy, region, account, and review requirements. Verify: [YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) and [expanded YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240).
- **Original/authentic value:** monetization review assesses the channel as a whole. Mass-produced/repetitive (“inauthentic”) or reused material without significant original commentary, modification, education, or entertainment can make the channel ineligible even when copyright permission exists. Templates and AI assistance do not remove the need for meaningful human authorship and variation. Verify: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).
- **AI/synthetic media:** creators must disclose realistic altered or synthetic content where viewers could mistake it for a real person, place, scene, or event. Production assistance such as ideation, scripts, captions, or clearly unrealistic effects generally does not itself require the altered-content disclosure. Sensitive topics may receive a more prominent label. Verify: [Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491).
- **Copyright:** permission, Creative Commons terms, public-domain status, or a legally supportable exception must be established asset by asset. Credit or “no infringement intended” does not create rights, and fair use/fair dealing is fact-specific. Use licensed music/B-roll and retain licenses. Verify: [Copyright on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466) and [fair use](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148).
- **Paid promotion:** disclose paid product placements, endorsements, sponsorships, and other commercial relationships using YouTube’s paid-promotion declaration and applicable local disclosure law; make an unambiguous audience-facing disclosure. Verify: [Add paid product placements, sponsorships & endorsements](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235).
- **Spam/deception/fake engagement:** misleading metadata, scams, sold engagement, sub4sub, and content whose sole purpose is to incentivize engagement are prohibited or risky. Calls to subscribe are allowed when not deceptive/manipulative. Verify: [Fake engagement policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767) and [spam, deceptive practices & scams](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973).
- **Security demonstrations:** do not facilitate harmful or dangerous conduct; use isolated labs, redact operational details, and focus on defense. Verify before every cyber episode: [Harmful or dangerous content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801964).

## 10. Seven-day launch checklist

**Day 1:** lock positioning, handle, owner roles, account recovery, two-step verification/passkeys, least-privilege channel permissions, and brand account custody.
**Day 2:** photograph/record founder assets; produce banner, avatar, lower thirds, end screen, source cards, caption style, and thumbnail templates.
**Day 3:** validate the lead magnet and privacy/consent/unsubscribe flow; create UTM links and a test contact; do not publish a broken funnel.
**Day 4:** record trailer and videos 1–3; capture clean audio twice; complete evidence registers and asset licences.
**Day 5:** edit, caption, transcript-check, mobile/TV QA, copyright check, and two thumbnail variants per launch video.
**Day 6:** upload unlisted; complete title/description/chapters/end screen/cards/disclosures; have a second human fact-check every claim and link.
**Day 7:** publish trailer + video 1 + two Shorts + welcome post; founder replies substantively for 45 minutes; log 2h/24h baseline without making premature changes.

## 11. 30/60/90-day execution

- **Day 0–30 — Prove the operating system:** publish 8 long-form, 20 Shorts, 4 posts, 4 lives. Establish baseline by pillar and source. Validate that every video has evidence, captions, a working CTA, and consented attribution. Gate: no backlog below two finished long-form episodes.
- **Day 31–60 — Concentrate:** publish the next 8/20/4/4. Launch one three-video series only if the two-signal expansion rule is met. Release the first paid template after five user interviews. Gate: testimonial/case claims require written permission and evidence.
- **Day 61–90 — Productize carefully:** publish final 8/20/4/5. Package a diagnostic workshop; prepare a one-page sponsor deck from verified audience data but do not accept a poor-fit sponsor. Conduct a 90-day retrospective and approve the next quarter using 28-day cohorts.

**Rollback:** unlist (do not hastily delete) a materially inaccurate video, preserve analytics/evidence, publish a correction, fix the source process, and only republish after approval. Stop a campaign immediately if consent, disclosure, rights, or security boundaries fail.
