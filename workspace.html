How it works

  1. Choose a plan and team size. Start the 14-day trial with no card charged.
  2. We set up your domain. Email, storage and calendars are configured for you during onboarding — you do not do this alone.
  3. Invite your people. Accounts are per person and can be reassigned when someone leaves.
  4. We keep it running. Updates, backups, spam filtering and security patching are handled for you.

Security and privacy

A productivity service holds your business's correspondence and files. That deserves plain answers, not badges.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

TLS 1.2 or better in transit, encryption at rest on stored files and mail, and session timeouts on inactivity.

Your data is yours

Export your mail, files and calendars at any time in standard formats. Cancelling does not hold your business records hostage.

Card details never touch us

Payments are processed by Stripe on their hosted page. ClearGlass never sees, stores or transmits a card number.

Access is per person

Every account is individual with its own credentials and multi-factor authentication. Shared logins are not required and not encouraged.

Canadian tax handled properly

GST/HST is calculated at checkout from your billing address and itemised on every invoice for your bookkeeping.

Written policies, not vibes

Read the privacy policy, terms, acceptable use and accessibility commitment before you buy, not after.

Frequently asked questions

Are these prices final?

No — they are approximate, quoted in Canadian dollars and shown before tax. Applicable GST/HST is calculated at checkout from your billing address. Prices are also subject to supplier pricing, licensing requirements, currency movement and payment-provider fees. We will tell you before any change affects a renewal.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Nothing is charged during the trial. At the end, the plan you chose begins billing at the number of people you set. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged at all.

Can I change the number of people mid-month?

Yes. People added mid-period bill pro-rata for the remainder. People removed credit against your next invoice rather than being refunded outright.

What is the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual is billed once for twelve months and works out about 17% cheaper — roughly two months free. Monthly is billed every month with no commitment and can be cancelled at any renewal.

Can I move my existing email and files across?

Yes. Migration from an existing provider is included with Complete and available as a one-time service on the other plans. We do the move with you rather than handing you a guide.

How do I cancel, and what is the refund position?

Cancel from your account in one step. Access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for. Refund and cancellation terms are set out in the terms of service.

Who is behind this?

ClearGlass Inc., an Ontario company. The same team that does the security work on this site runs the platform, which is why the security section above talks about specifics rather than logos.
