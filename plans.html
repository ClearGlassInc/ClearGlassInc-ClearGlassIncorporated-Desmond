How it works

  1. Pick a plan and seat count. Start on any tier; the trial runs 14 days.
  2. Invite your operators. Seats are assigned per person and can be reassigned.
  3. Connect your sources. We help wire your first feeds during onboarding.
  4. Change plans any time. Upgrades apply immediately and bill pro-rata; downgrades take effect at renewal.

Security and compliance

Guardian is a security product, so the platform is held to the standard it asks of you.

Every action is logged

Operator actions, system events and AI commands are written to an append-only ledger with severity and actor, so an audit can reconstruct who decided what.

Consequential actions are gated

Anything touching credentials, remote execution or data export is scored and routed to a human approval before it runs. Nothing consequential executes unattended.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

TLS 1.2 or better in transit, encryption at rest server-side, scoped sessions with inactivity timeout, and per-endpoint rate limiting.

Card data never touches us

Payments are processed by Stripe on their hosted page. ClearGlass never sees, stores or transmits a card number.

Canadian tax handled correctly

GST/HST is calculated at checkout from your billing address and itemised on every invoice.

Your data stays yours

Export your audit ledger and operational data at any time. Cancelling does not hold your records hostage.

Frequently asked questions

Are these prices final?

They are approximate and shown in Canadian dollars, before tax. Applicable GST/HST is calculated at checkout from your billing address. Amounts may change with currency conversion or payment-provider fees, and we will tell you before any change affects a renewal.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Nothing is charged during the trial. At the end, the plan you chose begins billing at the seat count you set. Cancel from the dashboard before the trial ends and you are not charged at all.

Can I change seats mid-month?

Yes. Added seats bill pro-rata for the remainder of the period. Removed seats credit against your next invoice rather than being refunded outright.

What is the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual is billed once for twelve months and works out about 16.7% cheaper — roughly two months free. Monthly is billed every month with no commitment and can be cancelled at any renewal.

Can I upgrade, downgrade or pause?

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal so you keep what you paid for. Pausing is available on annual plans for up to 60 days once per term.

How do I cancel?

From the billing section of your dashboard, in one step, with no phone call. Access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.

Do you offer a demo instead?

Yes. If you would rather see it driven by someone who knows it before committing a team to it, book a walkthrough and we will run your scenarios rather than a canned script.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us the size of your team and what you are trying to see, and we will tell you honestly which tier covers it — including when the answer is the cheapest one.
