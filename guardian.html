Trust and governance statements

Audit-ready control doctrine

  • Every model recommendation is treated as untrusted until verified against policy and evidence.
  • Human approval remains the authority boundary for critical actions and external execution.
  • Operator corrections become evaluated improvement candidates, not silent production mutations.
  • Each prompt, workflow, model route, and policy bundle is versioned, reviewed, and reversible.
  • Need-to-know access is enforced at row, column, entity, mission, and coalition compartments.
  • Audit logs preserve who saw what, what changed, which evidence was used, and why action was allowed.
  • Drift detection blocks degraded automation before degraded confidence becomes operational risk.
  • Deployment promotion requires test evidence, rollback paths, owner approval, and post-release monitoring.

Engineered motion language

  • Use a controlled cinematic boot sequence: loader, badge, headline, metrics, console, then architecture panels.
  • Reserve split-text reveals for the hero and command doctrine only; avoid animating dense technical copy.
  • Use transform and opacity for all section reveals, with glow changes limited to hierarchy and state.
