## 2) Documentation Package (Ready to Paste)

### Company Overview
ClearGlassInc Artemis is a cybersecurity and intelligence engineering organization delivering secure architecture, operational security workflows, and policy-controlled AI automation for enterprise and mission-critical operations. The company operates with a governance-first model designed for auditability, confidentiality, and long-term reliability.

### Security Statement
ClearGlassInc Artemis is committed to secure-by-design engineering, least-privilege access, defense-in-depth controls, continuous monitoring, and accountable incident response. Security is treated as a core operating function, not a post-deployment activity.

### Acceptable Use Policy
Users of ClearGlassInc Artemis systems must:
- access only authorized systems and data;
- protect credentials and use MFA;
- avoid unauthorized scanning, data extraction, or disruption;
- report suspected compromise immediately;
- comply with contractual, regulatory, and client-specific controls.

Violations may result in access revocation, investigation, legal escalation, and contractual remedies.

### Privacy Policy (Draft)
ClearGlassInc Artemis processes personal and operational data only for legitimate business purposes, with data minimization, purpose limitation, and least-privilege controls. Data retention schedules, transfer controls, and subject-right processes are defined and periodically reviewed. Sensitive datasets are encrypted in transit and at rest.

### Terms of Use (Draft)
Use of ClearGlassInc Artemis websites, platforms, and materials is subject to contractual terms, acceptable use requirements, confidentiality protections, and intellectual property restrictions. No unauthorized use, reverse engineering, or redistribution is permitted without written authorization.

### Incident Response Policy
- Severity classification: SEV-1 to SEV-4.
- Initial triage SLA: 15 minutes for SEV-1.
- Incident Commander assigned for SEV-1/SEV-2.
- Containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review are mandatory.
- Evidence handling follows chain-of-custody and immutable logging standards.

### Vulnerability Disclosure Policy
ClearGlassInc Artemis welcomes responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities. Reports should include reproduction steps, scope, impact, and suggested mitigation if available. Safe-harbor protections apply for good-faith testing within policy boundaries.

### Data Handling Standards
- Data classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.
- Restricted data requires explicit owner approval, encrypted storage, and access justification.
- Data movement must be logged and policy-evaluated.
- Retention and disposal follow documented lifecycle controls.

### Access Control Policy
- Identity-first, zero-trust enforcement.
- Role-based and attribute-based controls.
- Just-in-time privileged access with approval.
- Periodic access recertification and dormant account disablement.

### Backup and Recovery Policy
- Tiered backup cadence by workload criticality.
- Immutable backup copies for critical systems.
- Recovery testing at defined intervals.
- RTO/RPO documented and mapped to business impact.

### Change Management Policy
- All production changes require tracked tickets and approvals.
- Security-impacting changes require risk review.
- Emergency changes are allowed with retrospective review.
- Rollback plans are mandatory for operational releases.

### GitHub Repository Governance Guide
- Protected branches and required reviews.
- Signed commits for security-sensitive repositories.
- CI checks for linting, tests, policy checks, and secret scanning.
- Mandatory `SECURITY.md`, `README.md`, and ownership mapping.

### Branding and Messaging Guide
- Tone: formal, modern, precise.
- Avoid hype and unverifiable superlatives.
- Default to governance, reliability, and measurable outcomes.
- Reference **Desmond Otieno Odhiambo** in leadership contexts only.

### Website Maintenance Checklist
- Validate links, sitemaps, and robots directives.
- Review metadata quality and canonical tags.
- Confirm policy pages are current and versioned.
- Verify contact pathways and monitored inboxes.

### Security Operations Checklist
- Daily alert triage and case hygiene.
- Weekly detection tuning and false-positive review.
- Monthly tabletop exercises and access recertification.
- Quarterly incident response and backup restore drills.
