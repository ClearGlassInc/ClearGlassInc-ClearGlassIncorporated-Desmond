# ClearGlass Commercial Execution Scorecard

**Objective:** Build a repeatable path to CAD $1M annual revenue without confusing software activity with commercial execution.

## North-star math

Target: **CAD $1,000,000 annual revenue / ~CAD $83,333 MRR**.

A practical service-led path:

- 4 new qualified sales conversations/week
- 2 proposals/week once qualified
- Target initial engagement: CAD $249–$3,000+
- Convert successful assessments into implementation and recurring monitoring
- Target recurring base: CAD $50,000+ MRR before attempting aggressive software scale

## Offer ladder

1. **Security Quick-Audit — CAD $249 one-time**
   - Entry offer.
   - Deliver a concise evidence-based findings report and prioritized remediation list.

2. **M365 + Windows Hardening Sprint — from CAD $2,500**
   - Primary implementation upsell after a qualified finding.

3. **PHIPA Readiness — from CAD $3,000**
   - Compliance-focused assessment for appropriate organizations.

4. **Managed Monitoring — from CAD $600/month**
   - Recurring revenue path after an assessment/implementation.

5. **Critical Minerals Compliance Strategy — CAD $1,499 one-time**
   - Specialist offer; sell only where the prospect has a genuine fit.

## Daily execution rule

Before substantial product work, complete at least one revenue-producing action:

- qualified prospect conversation
- discovery call booked
- proposal sent
- payment received
- customer deliverable completed
- renewal/retention action completed

**Evidence is required.** Record the event in the pipeline rather than counting planning as execution.

## Commercial guardrails

- Do not claim a customer, payment, case study, certification, security finding, or result unless evidence exists.
- Do not invent urgency, vulnerabilities, savings, compliance status, or technical findings.
- Do not send bulk unsolicited commercial electronic messages. Canadian CASL requirements must be reviewed before outreach; maintain consent/relationship records and provide required identification and unsubscribe mechanisms.
- Do not build a new product merely because it is technically interesting. Build only when it improves conversion, delivery, retention, margin, or a validated customer requirement.

## Competitive positioning

ClearGlass should not compete by pretending to be a full-scale MSP/MSSP. Burlington already has established providers offering managed IT, cybersecurity, MDR, cloud and related services. Position ClearGlass around a focused wedge: **evidence-driven cybersecurity + AI governance + remediation for organizations that need a clear, executive-ready answer to what is exposed, why it matters, and what to do next.**

## 30-day execution sequence

### Week 1 — Prove the funnel
- Verify analytics.
- Verify every commercial CTA and checkout path.
- Sell the CAD $249 Quick-Audit.
- Create the first 20-prospect qualified list.
- Conduct targeted outreach only where lawful and appropriate.

### Week 2 — Sell and deliver
- Hold discovery conversations.
- Close the first Quick-Audits.
- Deliver reports quickly.
- Ask for permission to use anonymized outcomes as proof.

### Week 3 — Upsell from evidence
- Convert qualified findings into Hardening Sprint or PHIPA Readiness proposals.
- Offer Managed Monitoring only where there is a genuine recurring need.

### Week 4 — Systematize
- Measure conversion by source and offer.
- Calculate delivery hours and gross margin.
- Automate only repeated delivery/admin work.
- Kill channels that generate activity without qualified opportunities.

## Definition of done

The commercial system is working when ClearGlass can repeatedly demonstrate:

**qualified lead → conversation → paid entry offer → measurable outcome → implementation → recurring revenue → retention.**

Software supports this loop. It does not replace the selling.
