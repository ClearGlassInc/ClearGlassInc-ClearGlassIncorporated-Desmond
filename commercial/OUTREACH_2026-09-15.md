## 1. CASL compliance requirements (applies to every draft below)

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation governs commercial electronic messages sent to Canadian recipients. Every draft in this file is written to satisfy it, and must keep these elements when edited:

1. **Consent basis.** Use implied consent only where the business email address is conspicuously published on the company's own website without a statement refusing unsolicited commercial messages, and the message relates to that person's business role. Record the URL and the date it was observed, per recipient, before sending.
2. **Identification.** Sender name, company name, and mailing address appear in the message.
3. **Contact.** A working email address and phone number appear in the message.
4. **Unsubscribe.** A plain, no-cost opt-out that is honoured within 10 business days.
5. **Records.** Keep the consent basis, the observed URL, the observation date, the exact message sent, and any opt-out, for each recipient.

Do not bulk-send. Do not buy or scrape contact lists. Do not message a personal address where a business address exists.

## 5. Shared 20-minute fit-call agenda

Applies to every account above. The call is a qualification call. It is not a free consultation, and it is not the assessment.

```
0-2 min    What they do, in their words. Do not skip this to save time.
2-8 min    How work moves today. Lead or order in, through which systems, to
           delivered. Ask for the last real example, not the intended process.
8-13 min   Where it breaks. Where is work re-keyed, chased, or lost. Who notices
           first. What does it cost when it happens.
13-16 min  Any AI or automation in use, and who owns it when it misbehaves.
16-18 min  Whether the assessment fits. Say plainly if it does not.
18-20 min  Decision, budget authority, timing, and the specific next step with a
           date on it.
```

Outcome must be recorded as exactly one of: fit confirmed, not a fit, nurture, proposal approved for drafting, follow-up required.

Rules for the call:
- Do not quote the implementation sprint. Scope is unknown at this point.
- Do not assert any security finding. Nothing has been examined.
- Do not claim a client, a case study, or a result. None exist yet on record.
- If they are not a fit, say so in the call. It costs one opportunity and buys a referral.
