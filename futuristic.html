The concept

Depth you can feel, clarity you can trust.

Every surface is a pane of intelligent glass — refracting the light behind it, catching a specular streak as your cursor moves, and settling with physical inertia. The system reads like a premium instrument: cinematic, calm, never cluttered.

Five elevation levels, three blur tiers, and a single prism palette keep the whole world coherent. Motion is ambient by default and tactile on demand.
