## The call

What was decided, stated as a commitment, not a preference.

## The strongest alternative

The option that was genuinely competitive — not a strawman. Why a reasonable
engineer would have picked it.

## Reasoning at the time

The constraints, the unknowns, and the assumptions actually held on the decision
date. Written without hindsight.

## What actually happened

and the baseline. `measured` claims name their source system.

## What we got wrong

Mandatory. The assumption that did not hold, the cost that was underestimated,
or the failure mode that was not modelled. If nothing was wrong, the decision
was too small to journal.

Concrete: a code path, a gate, a runbook, a threshold, a team boundary.
