## LinkedIn — executive angle

Your security stack can be fully funded and your exposure can still be poorly mapped.

CDW Canada's 2026 study reports that large enterprises in its survey experienced 342 average cyberattacks, up from 191. That is a study finding—not a prediction for your organization.

The practical question is: **Can you demonstrate which endpoint, API token or AI agent can reach which critical resource—and who approved that access?**

We use five operating disciplines:
1. Discover endpoints, APIs, machine identities and agents continuously.
2. Assign identities and remove unnecessary privilege.
3. Enforce resource-level policy, not just network location.
4. Require independent human approval for high-impact agent actions.
5. Preserve decision evidence and retest the reduction.

This is the difference between buying controls and operating them.
