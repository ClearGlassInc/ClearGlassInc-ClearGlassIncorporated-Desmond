Two connected layers: the Agent and the Engine

The kit is more than documents. An adaptive Agent guides you to the right next step; a deterministic Engine turns that into structured, repeatable output. Same source of truth, every time.

Layer 01 · Adaptive

The Agent

Analysis, guidance, and adaptive recommendations — the layer a person talks to.

  • Reads your situation — business size, maturity, and the pain point you name.
  • Recommends the next move — which policy, which risk to fix first, what to say in an incident.
  • Explains in plain language — no jargon, one clear action, backed by the kit.
  • Stays honest — never invents controls, prices, or legal thresholds.
Layer 02 · Deterministic

The Engine

Orchestration, repeatable generation, and structured output — the layer that produces the deliverables.

  • Scores risk — likelihood × impact, banded Low → Critical, the same way every run.
  • Renders policies — eight templates personalised to your business name.
  • Assembles comms — incident scripts by phase and audience, with approval gates.
  • One source of truth — the page, the advisor, and the backend all read the same kit.

How the agent stays trustworthy

  • One source of truth. The advisor, this page, and the Python backend all read the same kit content.
  • No fabrication. Answers come from the kit's policies, risk model, scripts, and glossary — nothing invented.
  • Approval gates. Regulator and media messages are drafts that need human sign-off before they go out.
  • Not legal advice. Breach-notification calls (PIPEDA / PHIPA) go to a qualified advisor.

Built for the way small teams actually work: plain words, one clear next step, and templates you can copy in seconds. The full agent specification lives in agents/smb_cyber_trust_kit/.
