## 4. First-week execution plan

These are planning targets, not statutory or contractual deadlines. An actual urgent matter overrides this sequence.

### Day 1 — establish control
- Assign one authorized company-side matter owner and a backup.
- Obtain the exact legal name and primary registry source for each potentially relevant entity; do not infer status from branding or a website.
- Start an evidence index with source owner, capture date, provenance, access restriction, and next action.
- Ask counsel/leadership whether any demand, incident, proceeding, regulator contact, insurance-notice condition, or date-sensitive obligation is active. Escalate any yes immediately.
- Keep confidential materials out of public GitHub/Slack/issue trackers; place them only in an approved restricted system.

### Days 2–3 — assemble the minimum viable evidence pack
- Inventory current commercial agreements, IP/contractor terms, public claims, privacy/security commitments, model/vendor terms, insurance, and governance records.
- Map relevant entities, provinces/territories, counterparties, worker/customer locations, contract forum clauses, and data flows.
- Mark each item **located / not supplied / superseded / needs authentication**. Do not treat a draft as executed or a website statement as registry evidence.

### Days 3–4 — identify counsel coverage
- Define the actual matter(s), urgency, governing-law questions and the jurisdictions where advice is needed.
- Shortlist Ontario-licensed business counsel through the Law Society of Ontario directory; verify current practising status, practice area, identity, conflicts process, and secure intake channel.
- Add privacy/cybersecurity, IP/technology, employment, litigation/insurance, securities/tax or foreign counsel only where the evidence indicates a need.
- Do not assume a lawyer is available or has accepted the retainer until confirmed in writing.

### Days 4–5 — counsel-ready briefing and red-team
- Prepare a one-page issue statement, party/entity map, chronology, evidence index, top questions, known dates (each with source), and desired decisions.
- Ask the lead counsel coordinator to run the nine role checks sequentially if separate agents are unavailable.
- Have the independent reviewer record the three strongest objections and unresolved disagreements.
- Present recommendations, trade-offs, owners and approval gates to leadership. Do not execute legal positions until appropriate approval is documented.

### End of week — close only with evidence
- Approve a prioritized risk register with accountable owners and next-review dates.
- Record counsel questions and status as **unassigned / prepared / sent with authorization / answered / requires follow-up**. No communication is deemed sent merely because a draft exists.
- Establish metric definitions and baselines from actual evidence; leave unavailable metrics **NOT MEASURED**.

## 9. Scenario stress tests and unresolved disagreements

Run these once the appropriate evidence is available; do not treat these as incidents that have occurred.

- **Major customer dispute:** acceptance/payment evidence, limitations, notice and escalation clauses, versioned chronology, and counsel approval before communications.
- **Ransomware/security incident:** preserve logs and evidence, contain through authorized incident leadership, identify data/contracts/jurisdictions, and route notification/insurance decisions to counsel.
- **Contractor IP claim:** verify signed assignment/engagement terms, actual authorship and provenance, access history, licences, and asset chain of title.
- **Privacy complaint or incident:** map affected data, controller/processor roles where relevant, applicable regime, record-keeping, notification thresholds, and decision owner with counsel.
- **Misleading-marketing allegation:** identify the exact claim, audience, evidence, approvals and consumer/customer context; preserve the published version; counsel reviews any response.
- **Vendor outage/failure:** capture logs and notices, check SLA/termination/continuity clauses, dependencies, data retrieval and transition terms; do not threaten or terminate without authority.

For each scenario, the adversarial reviewer must identify: (1) the strongest contrary legal/factual interpretation; (2) the most consequential missing evidence; and (3) a credible way the proposed controls could fail. Record disagreements instead of forcing false consensus.
