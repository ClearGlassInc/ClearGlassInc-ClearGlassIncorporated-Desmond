
# Industrial and Nuclear Supplier Qualification Register

**Status:** Research baseline only — no supplier approval is claimed.
**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Purpose

Provide an evidence-backed qualification checklist for ClearGlass as it evaluates industrial and energy-sector supply-chain entry.

This register deliberately treats supplier registration, qualification, approval, and contract award as separate states.

## OPG — Ontario Power Generation

**Evidence status:** Public supplier requirements identified; ClearGlass approval is **NOT VERIFIED**.

OPG states that its Supply Chain develops supplier relationships against price, quality, delivery, and service-level requirements. OPG also requires suppliers, owners, employees, agents, partners, and subcontractors providing goods or services to follow its Supplier Code of Conduct.

Primary sources:

- https://www.opg.com/suppliers/supply-chain/
- https://www.opg.com/about-us/governance-regulation/code-of-business-conduct/
- https://www.opg.com/suppliers/labour-requirements/
- https://archive.opg.com/pdf_archive/Procurement%20Terms%20of%20Service/Respond%20to%20RFP%20Prerequisites.pdf

OPG procurement material documents use of SAP Ariba for RFP response workflows. Treat the specific RFP and purchase-order requirements as controlling for each opportunity.

### OPG checklist

- [ ] Identify the exact commodity/service category.
- [ ] Identify the procurement route and buyer.
- [ ] Confirm whether Ariba access or another registration route is required.
- [ ] Review the applicable supplier code.
- [ ] Review insurance, safety, labour, quality, and technical requirements for the specific scope.
- [ ] Confirm data, cybersecurity, screening, and site-access requirements where applicable.
- [ ] Maintain evidence of every submission and OPG response.
- [ ] Never label ClearGlass an OPG-approved supplier without authoritative confirmation.

## Bruce Power

**Evidence status:** Public supplier-prequalification structure identified; ClearGlass approval is **NOT VERIFIED**.

Bruce Power's Supplier Prequalification and Performance Portal uses ISNetworld to collect, review, and verify information including health and safety programs, environmental programs, insurance, workers' compensation, and safety statistics. Bruce Power states that its Approved Supplier List and Active Vendor List include more than 2,000 suppliers; the public page also states that suppliers requiring quality-assurance programs may be evaluated and audited and that extensive QA programs can take up to six months.

Primary sources:

- https://www.brucepower.com/become-a-supplier/prequalification-portal/
- https://www.brucepower.com/become-a-supplier/contracting-standards/
- https://www.brucepower.com/about-us/supply-chain/

### Bruce Power checklist

- [ ] Determine whether the proposed service falls within site-contractor/supplier scope.
- [ ] Assess ISNetworld registration requirements.
- [ ] Prepare health/safety/environmental evidence.
- [ ] Prepare insurance and workers' compensation evidence.
- [ ] Prepare safety-statistics evidence.
- [ ] Determine whether a quality-assurance program applies.
- [ ] Determine the applicable QA standard and audit route for the exact scope.
- [ ] Track qualification time and evidence.
- [ ] Never claim ASL/AVL status without authoritative confirmation.

## CNSC — Regulatory intelligence boundary

**Evidence status:** Public regulatory framework identified; ClearGlass is not a CNSC licensee by virtue of monitoring public material.

The CNSC describes its nuclear regulatory framework as including laws, regulations, licences, regulatory documents, and related instruments. CNSC public material also describes cybersecurity expectations relevant to nuclear facilities and references CSA N290.7 in its compliance-verification work for high-security nuclear facilities.

Primary sources:

- https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/acts-and-regulations/regulatory-documents/
- https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/acts-and-regulations/regulatory-framework/
- https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/nuclear-safety-security/science-research-health/scientific-technical-information/technical-papers-and-articles/2026/jung-cybercon26/
- https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/nuclear-safety-security/science-research-health/scientific-technical-information/technical-papers-and-articles/2026/ibrahim-cybercon26/
- https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/acts-and-regulations/consultation/profile/2-12-1-2026/

The CNSC consultation page currently lists REGDOC-2.12.1, General Nuclear Security, Version 1.0 as open from September 25 through December 23, 2026. That consultation status must be rechecked before relying on it for any later decision.

### CNSC monitoring rules

- [ ] Monitor only official public material unless separately authorized.
- [ ] Preserve source URL, retrieval timestamp, and content fingerprint.
- [ ] Classify publication, consultation, amendment, application, licensing decision, and guidance separately.
- [ ] Do not infer legal effect from an HTML change alone.
- [ ] Require human verification before commercial or compliance action.
- [ ] Do not handle prescribed/classified information through the public monitoring pipeline.

## Qualification state machine

Research
→ Requirement identified
→ Evidence package prepared
→ Registration/application submitted
→ Buyer/regulator acknowledgement
→ Qualification under review
→ Approved/qualified (ONLY with authoritative evidence)
→ Contract
→ Delivery
→ Performance evidence

No state may be skipped in company reporting.

## Evidence package

Each target should maintain:

- Organization name and exact legal entity.
- Procurement channel.
- Applicable supplier category.
- Requirement source.
- Publication date.
- Retrieval date.
- Submission date.
- Submission reference.
- Buyer/contact record where legitimately published.
- Current state.
- Next review date.
- Evidence URL or document identifier.
- Reviewer/owner.
- Notes on unresolved requirements.
