## Corrections to the record

1. **D4 does not block a reply to a firm that asked.** CASL section 6
   (consent, sender identification including the mailing address, and the
   unsubscribe mechanism) does not apply to a commercial electronic message
   "sent in response to a request, inquiry or complaint or is otherwise
   solicited by the person to whom the message is sent" (Electronic Commerce
   Protection Regulations, SOR/2013-221, s. 3(b); also the CRTC's CASL
   information session). The 1 October report said D4 blocks "every email,
   including a reply". It blocks cold email: new prospects and further
   unsolicited follow-ups. A firm that writes back with a question, or asks
   for details on a call, can be answered the same hour.
   [`../revenue-decisions.md`](../revenue-decisions.md) D4 and rule 7 of
   [`../../../offers/outreach/README.md`](../../../offers/outreach/README.md)
   now say so. `bots/outreach_preflight.py` is unchanged: it is a cold-email
   gate.
