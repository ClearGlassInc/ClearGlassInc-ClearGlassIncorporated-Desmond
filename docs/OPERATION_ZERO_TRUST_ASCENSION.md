## 1. Command intent

ClearGlass will build a commercially sustainable industrial technology and cybersecurity practice that can progressively qualify for higher-assurance energy and nuclear-sector work.

The strategic destination is industrial and energy infrastructure. The near-term operating requirement is revenue, customer evidence, delivery discipline, and qualification evidence.

### Non-negotiable authority boundaries

No automated agent may, without explicit human authorization:

- commit company funds or draw credit;
- sign contracts or accept legal liability;
- represent ClearGlass as an approved supplier when approval is not verified;
- connect to or modify production OT or safety-related systems;
- make binding legal, regulatory, customs, or nuclear determinations.

## 2. Decision Support Matrix

All triggers require evidence and management review. A single external signal must not cause an automatic 100% resource shift or credit draw.

| Trigger | Verification | Management action |
|---|---|---|
| US tariff scope expands | Confirm affected classifications, effective dates, exclusions, and official source | Expand tariff/supplier exposure analysis |
| Trade conditions improve | Confirm official measures and effective dates | Reassess cross-border customer opportunities |
| Commercial lending tightens | Confirm actual availability, covenants, pricing, and lender notices | Preserve liquidity; review financing alternatives |
| Nuclear/industrial procurement opportunity appears | Verify buyer, scope, eligibility, procurement route, security and quality requirements | Allocate controlled bid resources |
| Grid/edge hardware shortage | Verify current lead times and approved substitutes | Prefer customer-owned infrastructure or qualified alternatives |
| AI system benchmark degrades | Compare against labelled test set and acceptance threshold | Suspend affected automated decisions and require human review |
| Industrial cyber incident reported | Verify authorization, scope, evidence and engagement terms | Activate the documented incident-response process |

### Financial tripwires

These are internal control thresholds, not regulatory requirements.

- **Cash runway:** escalate below six months of essential operating expenditure.
- **Customer concentration:** escalate when any one customer exceeds 35% of contracted revenue.
- **Receivables:** escalate when material invoices pass contractual payment terms.
- **Unfunded delivery commitments:** do not accept work requiring material upfront expenditure without adequate financing or commercially acceptable payment terms.

## 8. Safety and scope gates

The initial industrial/nuclear demonstrations are **non-production, controlled, and read-only**.

The implementation must not:

- scan or modify a live OT or safety system without written authorization and an approved scope;
- ingest controlled nuclear information into an unapproved AI service;
- claim nuclear compliance based only on local hosting, encryption, or air-gapping;
- use rotating proxies to evade access controls or rate limits;
- classify a changed webpage as a confirmed regulatory event without human/source verification.
