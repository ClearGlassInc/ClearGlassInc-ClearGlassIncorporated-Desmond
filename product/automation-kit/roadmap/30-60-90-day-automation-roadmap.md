# 30/60/90-Day Automation Implementation Roadmap

## Days 0–30 — Discover and baseline

- Inventory candidate workflows.
- Document current-state steps and owners.
- Classify data and operational impact.
- Identify approval gates and evidence requirements.
- Select limited, reversible candidates for further evaluation.

## Days 31–60 — Controlled preparation

- Define future-state workflow diagrams or SOPs.
- Establish test cases and failure handling.
- Confirm access and data-minimization requirements.
- Prepare human-review procedures.
- Capture baseline measurements using documented assumptions.

## Days 61–90 — Review and controlled rollout preparation

- Review pilot evidence.
- Record exceptions and lessons learned.
- Update governance records.
- Confirm rollback/recovery procedures.
- Decide whether further implementation review is warranted.

## Operating rule

Each stage requires evidence and an accountable human owner. A failed or incomplete gate remains `NOT VERIFIED` until evidence closes it.

> This roadmap is a planning aid. It does not guarantee savings, ROI, compliance, security, adoption, or operational performance.
