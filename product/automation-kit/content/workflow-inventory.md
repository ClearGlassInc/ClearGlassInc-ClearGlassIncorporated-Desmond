## Review questions

1. Is the owner explicit?
2. Are sensitive inputs identified?
3. Is a human decision point required?
4. What evidence would show the workflow behaved as intended?
5. What happens when an automation step fails?
6. Can the change be reversed safely?

> This inventory is a planning and documentation aid, not a certification or assurance assessment.
