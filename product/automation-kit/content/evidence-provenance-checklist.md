# Evidence and Provenance Checklist

- [ ] Every material assertion has an identified source or is explicitly labeled as a planning assumption.
- [ ] Evidence location is recorded.
- [ ] Evidence timestamp is recorded when relevant.
- [ ] Inputs are attributable to an authorized source.
- [ ] Generated artifacts can be traced to the source inputs and version.
- [ ] Hashes or immutable references are used where appropriate.
- [ ] Human review is recorded for consequential outputs.
- [ ] Missing or conflicting evidence is marked `NOT VERIFIED`.
- [ ] No customer confidential information is embedded in examples.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, keys, or private repository material are embedded.
