# Automation Prioritization Matrix

Use the dimensions below to compare candidates transparently. Do not treat the result as a guaranteed business outcome.

| Dimension | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repetitive effort | Occasional | Recurring | Frequent |
| Process stability | Frequently changing | Moderately stable | Stable and documented |
| Reversibility | Difficult to reverse | Recoverable with effort | Easily reversible |
| Data sensitivity | Low sensitivity | Internal/confidential | Sensitive/high-impact |
| Operational impact | Limited | Material | Critical |
| Human judgment | Predominantly judgment-based | Mixed | Mostly rules/known criteria |
| Dependency complexity | Few dependencies | Several dependencies | Many/fragile dependencies |
