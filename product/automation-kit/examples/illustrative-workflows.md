# Original Illustrative Workflows

These examples are generic planning examples. They are not client workflows and contain no customer data, credentials, secrets, or proprietary third-party material.

## Example 1 — Intake triage

**Trigger:** A new internal request is submitted.

**Possible assistance:** Classify the request against predefined categories and prepare a draft routing recommendation.

**Human gate:** A designated owner confirms routing before any consequential action.

**Evidence:** Request ID, classification output, approver decision, timestamp.

## Example 2 — Document classification

**Trigger:** A document enters an approved internal repository.

**Possible assistance:** Extract non-sensitive metadata and suggest a category.

**Human gate:** Owner confirms category when classification affects retention, access, or downstream processing.

**Evidence:** Document reference, model/tool version, suggested category, reviewer decision.

## Example 3 — Reporting preparation

**Trigger:** A reporting period closes.

**Possible assistance:** Assemble approved source data into a draft report structure.

**Human gate:** Responsible reviewer checks source completeness and approves the final report.

**Evidence:** Source references, generation timestamp, reviewer approval, final artifact version.

## Example 4 — Approval routing

**Trigger:** A request reaches a defined decision threshold.

**Possible assistance:** Prepare a decision packet containing the documented inputs and known exceptions.

**Human gate:** Authorized human makes the consequential decision.

**Evidence:** Inputs, approval record, decision, and audit reference.

> These examples are illustrative planning material only and do not guarantee performance, compliance, security, or business outcomes.
