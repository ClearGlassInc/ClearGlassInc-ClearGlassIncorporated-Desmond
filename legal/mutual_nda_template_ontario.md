## 1. Purpose
The parties wish to evaluate and discuss potential business and technical collaboration (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" includes non-public business, technical, legal, financial, operational, and security information disclosed by either party, including platform information related to **ARTEMIS IV**, **WRAITH-OMEN**, and **AEGIS**.

## 3. Obligations
Each receiving party shall:
- use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
- protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care;
- restrict access to personnel/advisors with a need to know and equivalent confidentiality obligations;
- not disclose Confidential Information except as authorized in writing.

## 4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that is: publicly available without breach, independently developed, rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality, or approved for release in writing.

## 5. Term and Survival
- Agreement term: **three (3) years** from Effective Date.
- Confidentiality obligations survive for **five (5) years** after termination/expiry.

## 6. Required Disclosure
A receiving party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided prompt notice is given where legally permitted.

## 7. No License / No Warranty
No IP license is granted by disclosure except as expressly set out in writing. All Confidential Information is provided "as is."
