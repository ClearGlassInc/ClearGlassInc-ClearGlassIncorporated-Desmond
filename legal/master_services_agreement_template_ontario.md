**Brand Palette:** Navy `#0B1F3A`, Teal `#00A7A0`  
**Tagline:** **CLEARGLASS: See Through Everything**

> **Important:** This is a template for discussion purposes. Obtain Ontario legal counsel review before execution.

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") is effective as of **[Effective Date]** between:
- **ClearGlass Inc.** ("ClearGlass"), and
- **[Client Legal Name]** ("Client").

## 1. Scope and Structure
Services are provided under mutually executed Statements of Work ("SOWs") referencing this MSA. Each SOW defines deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, timeline, and commercial terms.

## 2. Fees and Payment
- Client shall pay fees as set out in applicable SOWs.
- Invoices are due within **thirty (30) days** unless otherwise stated.
- Overdue balances accrue interest at **1.5% per month** (or maximum lawful rate, if lower).

## 3. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property, deliverables, frameworks, models, code, documentation, and platform components created or provided by ClearGlass remain exclusively owned by ClearGlass unless expressly assigned in a signed writing.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will protect confidential information of the other party using reasonable safeguards and use it only as needed to perform obligations under this MSA and SOWs.

## 5. Warranties and Disclaimers
ClearGlass will perform services in a professional and workmanlike manner. Except as stated, services are provided "as is" without additional warranties.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, each party's aggregate liability under this MSA is limited to fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice. Client remains responsible for fees for services performed through termination date.

## 8. Governing Law and Venue
This MSA is governed by Ontario law and applicable federal law of Canada. The parties attorn to courts in Ontario.
