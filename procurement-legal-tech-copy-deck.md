## 5. Advanced feature lines (12)

1. **Autonomous intake & triage** — Inbound RFPs, tenders, contracts, and
   requests are parsed, classified, and routed by obligation type, deadline, and
   risk exposure, each with a structured summary and a proposed next action.
2. **Solicitation & requirement extraction** — Mandatory criteria, evaluation
   weightings, submission format, and compliance clauses are pulled verbatim
   from tender documents and mapped to a response matrix.
3. **Supplier identity & eligibility verification** — Counterparties are checked
   against registry, sanctions, debarment, and beneficial-ownership sources
   before engagement; unverifiable identities are held, not passed through.
4. **Contract drafting & clause assembly** — Agreements are built from an
   approved clause library with jurisdiction-aware fallbacks; every clause
   carries its provenance, and no bespoke legal term ships without sign-off.
5. **Obligation & renewal tracking** — Deliverables, milestones, indemnities,
   auto-renewals, and notice windows become a live register that raises the
   owner ahead of every deadline.
6. **Evidence capture & provenance** — Every document, decision, and data point
   is hashed, timestamped, and bound to its origin, producing a tamper-evident
   chain that stands up under audit and disclosure.
7. **Compliance mapping & control checks** — Workflows are tested continuously
   against applicable frameworks and flagged the moment an action would fall
   outside them.
8. **Approval orchestration** — Consequential actions route to the correct
   authority with full context, hold in a pending state, and release only on a
   recorded approval; thresholds and delegations are enforced in code.
9. **Reconciliation & discrepancy detection** — Invoices, purchase orders,
   receipts, and contract terms are matched three-way; variances outside
   tolerance are surfaced with evidence rather than silently accepted.
10. **Disclosure & records assembly** — On an access, audit, litigation-hold, or
    FOI request, the record set is assembled with provenance intact and
    redactions logged.
11. **Decision support & scoring** — Bids are scored against the published
    evaluation matrix with reasoning shown line by line; the system recommends,
    the evaluator decides and signs.
12. **Executive reporting & ledger export** — A standing report reconstructs
    what was decided, by whom, on what evidence, under which control —
    exportable to GRC, records, and finance systems without rekeying.

## 6. Control-rule statements (8)

1. **Low-risk work auto-executes and is logged.** Drafting, reading metrics,
   extraction, and reconciliation run automatically and land in the ledger.
2. **Content and non-price changes queue for review.** Publishing a response or
   editing a non-financial term is drafted in full and held for a named reviewer.
3. **Money, price, and legal position are blocked until approved.** Awards,
   payments, pricing, refunds, execution, and mass outbound are blocked until an
   approval record reaches `approved`.
4. **No fabrication of fact.** The system never invents a supplier,
   certification, price, review, or sense of urgency; unknowns are reported as
   unknown.
5. **Every material change is written to an append-only ledger** with actor,
   timestamp, evidence reference, and risk score.
6. **Fail closed, not open.** Missing authority, unverifiable identity, a broken
   evidence chain, or an unreachable control halts the workflow and escalates.
7. **Least privilege, scoped access.** Agents hold only the credentials a task
   requires, for only as long as it runs; write access is narrow and revocable.
8. **Human authority is always reversible-forward.** An operator can override,
   pause, or roll back any agent decision — and the override is itself logged.

## 7. Technology stack (futuristic but credible)

| Layer | Role | Design guarantee |
|-------|------|------------------|
| **Governance Core** | Scores and routes every proposed action to auto / queue / block | Deterministic, dependency-light, self-checks on boot; no action reaches an external system without passing through it |
| **Evidence Ledger** | Append-only, hash-linked event store | Immutable history; later alteration is detectable; exports to GRC |
| **Agent Mesh** | Task-specialized, supervised agents | Adapt to case variety, never to their own authority; capability is granted, not assumed |
| **Verification Fabric** | Identity and provenance before trust | Registry/sanctions checks + chain-of-custody; unverified inputs quarantined |
| **Secure Control Plane** | API and access boundary | Authn per call, secrets at runtime, safe mock-mode default, Canadian-hosted option |

Design goal: **resilience under partial failure** — any one layer can degrade
without a consequential action slipping through unapproved or unlogged.

## 8. Trust & verification

- **Identity verification** — No counterparty trusted on assertion alone;
  registry, sanctions, debarment, and authority-to-sign checks before entry;
  unverifiable identities held for human review.
- **Evidence-chain integrity** — Hash-linked, append-only history; provenance
  bound to every document and decision; redactions logged, never silently
  applied.
- **Auditability & disclosure** — Actor, timestamp, and risk score on every
  action; litigation-hold and FOI assembly; export to GRC, records, and finance.
- **Human oversight** — Named approver on every high-risk action; override,
  pause, and rollback at any point; delegations and thresholds enforced in code.

**Proof is the product.** In regulated work, the output is only as valuable as
your ability to defend it.
