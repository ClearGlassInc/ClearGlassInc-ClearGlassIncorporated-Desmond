# ClearGlass Inc. — Competitive and Search-Intent Scan

**Observed:** 9 October 2026  
**Use:** Internal strategy input. Not a market-size study, search-volume report, ranking report, or endorsement of competitor claims.  
**Primary hypothesis to validate:** Ontario small and midsize organizations need a clear, low-friction way to understand security exposure and practical next steps before commissioning a larger implementation.

## What the current market signals

| Provider / source | Publicly communicated entry point | Relevant signal | Implication for ClearGlass |
|---|---|---|---|
| [BotLogix — Ontario AI consulting](https://botlogix.ca/) | Free 30-minute discovery call; a stated $1,000 + HST AI Strategy Day | Local competitor explains the sequence: bring a real workflow, determine what is worth changing, then scope delivery. | Lead with a specific buyer problem and a defined first step; make the first conversation easy to understand. |

| [Proving Ground Consulting](https://provinggroundconsulting.ca/) | Readiness scorecard and short consultation | Offers a diagnostic before implementation; describes define → pilot → measure → decide. | Provide genuinely useful, low-friction educational value and explain how a first engagement reduces uncertainty. |
| [Fusion Computing — cybersecurity assessment](https://fusioncomputing.ca/cybersecurity-assessment/) | Free self-check; published typical range of $2,500–$6,500 for a paid assessment | Separates a self-reported score from a paid evidence-based assessment and makes the distinction explicit. | Differentiate the CAD $249 Security Quick-Audit by its evidence-limited, read-only scope—not by implying it is a penetration test, certification, or full enterprise audit. |
| [Canadian Centre for Cyber Security — SMB baseline controls](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/baseline-cyber-security-controls-small-and-medium-organizations) | Free government baseline guidance | A useful authoritative reference for educational content and practical control-oriented language. | Ground educational material in public authoritative guidance, then explain where a scoped service adds evidence review and prioritization. |

Competitor pricing and product claims above are what the referenced public pages communicated when reviewed; they have not been independently audited. Recheck each source before using comparative statements in public content. Do not claim that ClearGlass is cheaper, faster, more secure, or superior without comparable evidence.

## Recommended positioning and funnel

**One primary conversion path:** homepage → CAD $249 Security Quick-Audit page → Stripe checkout or 30-minute scoping call → written scope and authorization → evidence collection → report and review → separately approved remediation engagement if appropriate.

**What to emphasize:** low-friction entry, written scope, read-only posture, explicit authorization, evidence limitations, ranked findings, plain-language next steps, and a target delivery date confirmed after scope/evidence readiness.

**What not to promise:** guaranteed risk reduction, certification, compliance attestation, pentest coverage, a pass/fail security verdict based on missing evidence, three-day delivery before access/scope is ready, or a customer outcome without evidence.

### Search and content actions

1. Validate queries and page performance in Google Search Console or another authorized search dataset. Until access is confirmed, do not report keyword volumes, rankings, or organic growth.
2. Review every candidate URL for uniqueness, factual accuracy, page purpose, intent match, and a clear internal link to the relevant service. Do not create thin city pages.
3. Keep title tags descriptive and concise; Google may construct title links from title text, headings, and other prominent page text. Reference: [Google Search Central — title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).
4. Use the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance as a public educational source, with attribution, while keeping ClearGlass service descriptions distinct from government recommendations.
5. Review the production page and GitHub source separately before release. The live Quick-Audit page reviewed on 9 October 2026 presents a more specific buyer, scope, and process than the GitHub `main` version retrieved during this work. This source/deployment difference must be reconciled before merging or deploying homepage copy changes.

## Measurement plan

For the first four weeks, optimize for **qualified commercial evidence**, not vanity reach: qualified inquiries, inquiry source, booked and held conversations, proposals, signed engagements, cash collected, time-to-response, and delivery margin. Record the definition and reporting period for every metric. Keep unknown values marked `Unknown`; do not substitute zero when no data was collected.
